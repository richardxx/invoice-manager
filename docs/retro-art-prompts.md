# Retro artwork prompt set

The seven PNG assets in `src/main/resources/art/` were generated with the built-in image generation tool. Text, controls, progress, captions, credits, and version are rendered by Swing, not baked into the illustrations.

Common style for all six About scenes: late-1990s Windows shareware cartoon and 1998 computer-magazine comic, thick ink outlines, subtle halftone texture, limited teal, cream, coral, and navy palette. The recurring protagonist is a Chinese office worker with short spiky black hair, white shirt, and red tie. Wide 4:3 composition, expressive humor, no readable text, no speech bubbles, no watermark.

| Asset | Scene prompt |
| --- | --- |
| `splash.png` | Overworked office worker at a chunky beige CRT; paper invoices fly into a neat blue filing cabinet marked by pictograms; a sunbeam and relieved expression. Leave clear space at the bottom for app-rendered title and progress bar. |
| `about-1.png` | Coworkers crowd the desk with different crumpled invoices and reimbursement questions; the protagonist is overwhelmed. |
| `about-2.png` | Late-night office, protagonist buried under a mountain of invoices, calculator, folders, and a comical paper avalanche. |
| `about-3.png` | Protagonist discovers a helpful glowing coding assistant on the CRT, represented by a friendly blue cursor character; scattered invoices remain, but there is a new idea. |
| `about-4.png` | Protagonist and cursor assistant design the invoice software together, with invoice pool, people, and monthly-matrix icons visible on a beige CRT. |
| `about-5.png` | Finished software sorts scattered receipts into categories; coworkers and protagonist are pleased, with an orderly icon grid on the CRT. |
| `about-6.png` | Protagonist relaxes with tea at a clean desk beside the CRT; organized reimbursements, sunset, and the waving blue cursor assistant convey relief. |
